\section{Welcome Letter from the Chairs}

\begin{chairletter}{Anastasiia Bolkhovitina}
I am so excited to welcome you to this committee. Currently, I am a third-year student in IE, and I have been an ``MUN kid'' since I was 12 years old. My journey spanned from being a Secretary-General of the first international conference at my Ukrainian high-school to representing IE at Navarra and Harvard Model of United Nations (overall I did more than 30 simulations, crisis most recent years, so I appreciate the chaos being well-done). I also have participated in the European Youth Parliament, which I hope will come handy in this committee too, especially its deep emphasis on understanding the problem first.

The INTERPOL topic is especially meaningful to me, since I chose to pursue a degree in Economics and International Relations, out of a stubborn belief that diplomacy is mostly economics wearing a nicer suit. So please don't just arrive with pre-written speeches. Come ready to think on your feet, to adapt, to negotiate, and sometimes to change your mind when the room gives you a reason to. We are far less interested in surface-level debate than in seeing you wrestle with how genuinely complicated this issue is. And remember that we are here to help you do exactly that, so reach out any time you have a question or even just a doubt you want to talk through.

\signoff[Looking forward to meet you all,]{Anastasiia}
\end{chairletter}
