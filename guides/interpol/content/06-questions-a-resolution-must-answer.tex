\section{Questions a Resolution Must Answer}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item How should INTERPOL's mandate be clarified or expanded to address financial crime, and what institutional safeguards should govern any such expansion?
  \item What standards should govern the international disclosure of beneficial ownership information, and what consequences should follow for non-compliant jurisdictions?
  \item How should the tension between national financial sovereignty and international transparency obligations be resolved?
  \item What mechanisms should govern information sharing between INTERPOL, national financial intelligence units, and multilateral institutions such as FATF?
  \item How should the committee address the role of professional enablers in facilitating illicit financial flows?
  \item What governance reforms are necessary to ensure that financial crime enforcement is not applied selectively along geopolitical lines?
\end{enumerate}
