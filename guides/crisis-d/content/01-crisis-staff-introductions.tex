\begin{chairletter}[Chair]{Eva Dubost}
Hello everyone! I'm Eva, a third-year dual degree student in Political Science at Sciences Po Bordeaux and Hong Kong Baptist University, majoring in Global and China Studies. I was born in Madrid (so coming back feels like home), and my life has been anything but stationary. I'm French by origin, but grew up between Hong Kong, Singapore, and Milan before eventually landing in Bordeaux, which means I've spent more time packing suitcases than unpacking them ! My crisis journey started three years ago, at ESPOMUN in Lille, first as a Double Delegation delegate in the historical crisis: WWI Premises, where my partner and I went home with Best Delegate, and then on the other side as ASG Academics, helping conceptualise the crisis committees. Since then, I've had the chance to design crisis simulations as President of HKBUMUN, and this year served as Secretary General of HKBUMUN Conference 2026, running the conference in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese. I'm genuinely looking forward to sharing this experience with all of you. Outside of MUN, you'll find me reading, travelling, and discovering new places and ideas, basically anything that feeds my curiosity!

Can't wait to see you all in Madrid!

\end{chairletter}
