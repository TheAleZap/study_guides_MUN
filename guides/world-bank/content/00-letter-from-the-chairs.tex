\section{Letter from the Chairs}

\begin{chairletter}[Head Chair \& Co-Chair, World Bank Committee, IEUMUN 2026]{Nils Bischof \& Julius Masrouki}
Dear Delegates,

We, Nils and Julius, are thrilled to welcome you to the first-ever World Bank (WB) committee at IEUMUN 2026. It is truly an honour to serve you as chairs, and we are excited to guide you through a topic that we believe deserves far more attention than it gets: sovereign debt distress in developing economies. We have put a great deal of thought and care into writing this study guide for a committee that is academically rigorous and genuinely enjoyable to debate in, and we can’t wait to dive into this topic with you.

Hi there, my name is Nils Bischof, I’m 21 years old, and it is my pleasure to be your Head Chair for the World Bank committee this year! Originally from Germany, I grew up between New York and Frankfurt and I’m now heading into the third year of my bachelor’s degree in London, where I study and chase my passion for finance. I started my MUN journey two years ago, and IEUMUN 2024 marked my second conference. By now, I am lucky to say that I have been able to participate in MUN conferences all around the world. I like to call the World Bank my “home turf,” as I have delegated in it myself multiple times, most recently earlier this year at Harvard WorldMUN in Peru. Taking on this committee lets me combine my passion for finance with politics, and this year, land on a topic sitting right at that intersection: sovereign debt distress in developing economies. Outside all that, I enjoy going to the gym, brushing up my Spanish skills from time to time, and going on road trips with friends.

Hi everyone, my name is Julius Masrouki, and I’m delighted to be your Co-Chair for the World Bank Committee this year! I’m currently pursuing a BBA at Hult International Business School, where my interests span finance, entrepreneurship, business development, and international affairs, and outside of university I’ve picked up experience in sales and investment-related projects. What drew me to MUN was the mix of diplomacy, strategy, negotiation, and public speaking it demands, along with the chance to meet people from completely different backgrounds. I attended IEUMUN 2025 as a delegate, so it means a great deal to me to come back this year on the other side of the table as Chair. In my free time, I enjoy swimming, training and occasionally practicing martial arts. I’m also a big car enthusiast so I spend probably a little too much time driving around, which, given the current gas prices, might not be my smartest hobby financially.

Above all, our goal for this committee is high-quality debate: we want you to challenge each other’s views, think critically, and push past the positions you walked in with, while having fun along the way. As chairs, we want to build a space where you feel comfortable stepping outside your comfort zone, and where delegates learn from each other. We’re here to support you every step of the way, so don’t hesitate to reach out if you ever need guidance (\email{nilsbischof@web.de} \& \email{julius@masrouki.com}). We can’t wait to meet you all and see where you take this committee.

\signoff[Best,]{Nils \& Julius}
\end{chairletter}
