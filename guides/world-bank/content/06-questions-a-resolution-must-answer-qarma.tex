\section{Questions a Resolution Must Answer (QARMAs)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What should the Bank do with its own tools to push creditors toward the table?
  \item How can the Bank balance financing early, when it is most effective, against the risk of paying off other creditors that refuse comparable treatment?
  \item How should DSAs shape who gets what, under what constraints should access become easier or harder for countries under debt stress, and how would this be funded?
  \item How should debt transparency be handled, and what should the Bank do about countries that hide their debt?
  \item How much risk should the Bank take onto its own balance sheet while protecting its own lending capacity?
  \item What role should the Bank’s capacities as data provider, standard-setter, and policy advisor have in directing governments towards more sustainable debt management?
\end{enumerate}
