\section{Letter from the Chairs}

\begin{chairletter}{Nia \& Valentina}
Dear Delegates,

We are delighted to welcome you to the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) Committee at IEUMUN 2026. We are very excited to be your Chairs during this conference full of debate, challenges and, hopefully, a lasting experience for all of you. We promise to be there to guide you and help you progress during these inspiring four days. As you step into this journey, we encourage you to take every moment to show and enhance your debating skills, and, most importantly, to have fun in the process.

My name is Nia, and I am honoured to be one of your Chairs for this conference. I am a second-year student of the dual degree in Law and International Relations. I am originally from Bulgaria, but I study at IE University in Madrid. I have been passionate for MUNs since I joined university, and I love the feeling of personal growth and success it has given me, along with all the intelligent people I have met along the way. I am very eager to meet all of you, listen to your speeches, watch you debate, and, hopefully, find a great and lasting solution for this complex topic.

My name is Valentina, and it is a true pleasure to be one of your Chairs. I am a fourth-year International Relations student at Universidad Nebrija in Madrid.  I am originally from Mexico city and am genuinely excited to share these days of debate with all of you. I hope you will raise your placards without hesitation, because every person in this committee has something unique and valuable to contribute to the discussion. Do not be afraid to speak, to question, to challenge and to negotiate. We will do everything to ensure the committee is safe for everyone. We cannot wait to see what you bring to the table.

\end{chairletter}
