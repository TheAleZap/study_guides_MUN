\section{Questions a Resolution Must Answer}

\begin{itemize}
  \item How can the international community overcome UNESCO's financial, operational, and institutional limitations to improve the protection of cultural heritage in conflict-affected areas?
  \item How can the international community effectively document violations, preserve evidence, and ensure accountability for the destruction of cultural heritage while an armed conflict is ongoing?
  \item Are the existing international legal mechanisms sufficient to deter and prosecute the intentional destruction of cultural heritage, or should additional accountability mechanisms be developed?
  \item What concrete mechanisms can UNESCO and the international community establish to ensure rapid, well-funded and coordinated emergency responses to heritage destruction in active conflict zones?
  \item How can the international community develop reliable systems for documenting and preserving evidence of heritage destruction during ongoing armed conflicts, so that this evidence can effectively support future accountability and prosecution proceedings?
  \item How should the international community extend accountability for cultural heritage destruction to both non-state armed groups and state forces responsible for the deliberate targeting or systematic neglect of heritage?
  \item What measures can the international community adopt to ensure that accountability mechanisms for heritage destruction are applied consistently and universally?
\end{itemize}
