\section{Questions a Resolution Must Answer}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item How should the Security Council respond to attacks on commercial shipping and critical maritime infrastructure?
  \item How can the international community enhance the resilience of critical maritime infrastructure and global supply chains against military, environmental, and operational disruptions while ensuring the continued flow of international trade?
  \item How should the Security Council coordinate canal states, coastal states and shipping nations in safeguarding strategic maritime chokepoints?
  \item How can the Security Council use international law, particularly UNCLOS, to prevent maritime disputes from escalating into conflict?
  \item How can the Security Council strengthen the resilience of global trade and energy routes against conflict, attacks and other security threats?
\end{enumerate}
