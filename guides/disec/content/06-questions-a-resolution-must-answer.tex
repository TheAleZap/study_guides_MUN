\section{Questions a Resolution Must Answer}

\lead{The following questions address the range of subtopics that should be taken into consideration during the debate and resolution-writing processes:}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{} How can the international community better address the root causes of child soldier recruitment? These usually include but are not limited to poverty, instability, and limited educational and professional opportunities.
  \item \textbf{} What new mechanisms could be put in place in order to support the immediate identification and disarmament of child soldiers and how can chances of re-recruitment be mitigated?
  \item \textbf{} How can educational, professional, and recreational opportunities be improved in order to prevent the voluntary enrollment into armed groups by children as well as to guarantee the economic and social reintegration of former child soldiers?
  \item \textbf{} How can reintegration programs better deal with the separate physical and psychological needs of boy and girl child soldiers?
  \item \textbf{} How can emerging technologies be ethically and successfully relied on in order to identify, track, and protect formerly recruited child soldiers?
  \item \textbf{} How can the international community make sure that non-state armed groups who typically use and recruit children to partake in armed conflicts are effectively held accountable under international law?
  \item \textbf{} How can the international community ensure that current frameworks remain effective and applied in practice?
\end{enumerate}
