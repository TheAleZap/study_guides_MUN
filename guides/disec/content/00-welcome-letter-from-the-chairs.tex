\section{Welcome Letter from the Chairs}

\begin{chairletter}{The DISEC Chairs}
Dear Delegates,

It is our pleasure to welcome you to IEUMUN 2026 and, in particular, to the Disarmament and International Security Committee (DISEC). Before we proceed, we would like to briefly introduce ourselves and remind you that you can always contact us in order to clarify any doubts that you might have about the conference.

\minorheading{Arturs Juzenko}

I am originally from Latvia and am currently pursuing a degree in Finance and Marketing at Hult International Business School in London. I came across MUN during my first year of university and have been hooked ever since, participating in and chairing conferences across Europe and beyond. At the moment, I lead our team's bid to host WorldMUN in Riga 2027, and earlier this year I had the privilege of helping our delegation take Best Large Delegation at Harvard WorldMUN. Before MUN took over my life, I competed in judo as a part of our national team, so I know a thing or two about staying composed under pressure. MUN has been one of the most meaningful parts of my university experience, and I really hope you come out of this conference with great memories and a deeper understanding of the issues we will be debating. You are always welcome to reach out if you need any guidance while you prepare. I am really looking forward to meeting you all in person. You can contact me at: \email{arturs.juzenko@gmail.com}

\minorheading{Nathan Mahagamage}

I am from the tropical island nation of Sri Lanka and am currently doing my third year in Law at IE. I joined MUN through the IE Debate Club during my first year with the goal of competing in many conferences not only in Spain, but around the world. Since I started my journey 2 years ago, I have had the privilege of taking part in many conferences and meeting many interesting delegates. My most rewarding experience was winning an Honourable Mention in the Harvard National Model United Nations 2026 and being part of the IE delegation, where we won Outstanding Large Delegation, alongside Raquel as a team! Overall, the most fruitful part of my experience in MUN was the great talent and friendships I saw and made along the way. I am confident you will also feel the same way through your MUN journey and coming out of IE MUN. I am so looking forward to meeting you and wish you the best of luck for your preparation. Please feel free to reach out with any doubts or questions you may have at: \email{mmarioperera.ieu2023@student.ie.edu}
