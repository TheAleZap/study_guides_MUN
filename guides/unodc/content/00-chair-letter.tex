\section{Chair Letter}

\begin{chairletter}{Julia, Roshni and Francesca}
Dear distinguished delegates,

It is a great honour for us to host you for the IE University Model United Nations (IEUMUN) as your Chairs for the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime.

Hi, our names are Julia, Roshni, and Francesca, and we’ll be your Chairs for this committee.

Julia is a third-year student at Leiden University pursuing a Bachelor of International Relations and Organisations; her passion for Model UN began at the real UNODC Youth Forum in 2023 in Vienna, where she represented the Russian Federation and served as a final statement speaker before the 66th Commission on Narcotic Drugs, and being a UNODC Chair now holds deep symbolic meaning for her. Roshni is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Business Administration with a major in Finance at Hult International Business School, and Model United Nations has become one of her greatest passions in recent years, helping her develop her leadership, diplomacy, and public speaking skills. Francesca studies International Relations and International Law at the University of Edinburgh, Scotland, and is deeply passionate about how our world operates and how power rebalances through conflict and its impact on innocent civilian lives. Together, we hope to create a committee that is engaging, respectful, and intellectually stimulating, where every delegate feels encouraged to participate, think critically, and explore these topics with us through thoughtful and conscientious discussion.

We’ve noticed that many experienced delegates tend to just skim through background guides in their entirety, but for those just beginning their MUN journey, this approach doesn’t work as well - a study guide is meant to be your kickstart point for deeper research, not a shortcut past it.

You will have to learn and understand the game: the logic, the performance, and the careful act of balancing public statements and backchannel diplomacy. Eventually, you may find that the debates and preparation tend to blur together. No. That is not so. Therefore, if you are reading this letter - well done. You may already be one step ahead. And if you are not reading this letter, well, bear the challenge. Game on.

We would now like to reiterate the importance of content and bloc positions. Your words and actions should clearly reflect your state’s foreign policy and beyond that, there should exist little fluff in your speeches. As well, we encourage you to take risks. Raise your placard when nobody else dares to speak. Anyone can speak. Influence comes from those who know how to convey the meaning. Good luck to all delegates!

We look forward to meeting you!

\signoff[Yours sincerely,]{Julia, Roshni and Francesca}
\end{chairletter}
