\section{Questions a Resolution Must Answer}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{1.     How can the United States reconcile the recognition of private property rights in extracted space resources with the non-appropriation principle enshrined in Article II of the Outer Space Treaty?}
  \item \emph{2.     What regulatory body or bodies should be responsible for authorising novel private-sector space activities that do not fall within existing licensing frameworks (e.g., in-space manufacturing, debris removal, resource utilisation)?}
  \item \emph{3.     How should orbital debris and space traffic be managed as the number of commercial satellites and objects in orbit continues to grow? Should this be a national or international responsibility?}
  \item \emph{4.     How can the United States ensure that the commercialisation of outer space delivers equitable benefits to all nations, including non-spacefaring States, and avoids a "first-come, first-served" regime?}
  \item \emph{5.  What role should international cooperation and multilateral treaty-making play relative to national legislation in governing commercial space activities?}
  \item \emph{6.  How should Congress balance the imperative of encouraging private innovation and investment in space with the need to protect the space environment and celestial bodies from irreversible harm?}
  \item \emph{7.  Should the United States seek to amend or supplement existing international space-law instruments to provide clearer rules for commercial activities, or is a unilateral/plurilateral approach (such as the Artemis Accords) preferable?}
  \item \emph{8.  What governance frameworks are appropriate for permanent human settlement on celestial bodies, and how should sovereignty, jurisdiction, and civil rights be addressed in such settlements?}
  \item \emph{9.  How should liability for damage caused by commercial space activities be apportioned, particularly in an era of on-orbit servicing, active debris removal, and mega-constellations?}
  \item \emph{10.  To what extent does describing the commercialisation of outer space as "colonisation" raise ethical concerns, and how should these inform legislative action?}
\end{enumerate}
