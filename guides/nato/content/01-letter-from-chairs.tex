\section{Letter from Chairs}

\begin{chairletter}{Lucía Sobrado Louzao, Caspar Nitschke, Marie Matouchová}
We welcome you to IEUMUN 2026 and, in particular, to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, a forum and space in which dialogue, commitment, and cooperation become the keys to addressing challenges of our time, where the main topic that is going to be discussed is “Undersea Infrastructure Security in the Baltic Sea”. Next, the committee's chair team will present themselves to the committee. This team is formed by Lucía Sobrado Louzao, Caspar Maurice Nitschke and Marie Matouchová.

Lucía is a second-year student at IE University in Law and International Relations, as well as an IB graduate from United World College Red Cross Nordic in Norway. She was a chair for IEUMUN 2025 in DISEC, and she has been a delegate in more than 20 MUNs, including Harvard National Model UN and London Model UN, both as a double delegation. Lucía is extremely excited to chair this committee and learn from all of you! If you want to contact her, write an email to \email{lsobrado.ieu2024@student.ie.edu}

Caspar is a business student at Hult International Business School in London. He came into IEUMUN last year as a delegate, which is part of why he is back, this time on the other side of the dais. He has also represented Harvard WorldMUN, and at the London International Model UN. Away from committee, Caspar spends most of his time on the entrepreneurial side of things, working in marketing and building projects of his own while studying in London, which is where he first got hooked on the blend of fast thinking, persuasion, and negotiation that MUN runs on. He is looking forward to seeing what this committee makes of the topic. If you want to reach him, write to: \email{caspar.nitschke@icloud.com}

Marie is a second-year bachelor’s student at Copenhagen Business School, studying Business Administration and Digital Management. In fact, her first university-level MUN has been IEUMUN. Since then, she has gained more experience not only as a chair but also as an Academic Director at CBSMUN 2026. Marie is very much looking forward to seeing you in beautiful Madrid.

To help you prepare for the conference, we have drafted a study guide that we encourage you to review. Additionally, please consider the guiding questions for your position papers, and feel free to also consult the additional reading sources we provide. Should any questions arise, please do not hesitate to reach out to us

\signoff{Lucía Sobrado Louzao, Caspar Nitschke, Marie Matouchová}
\end{chairletter}
